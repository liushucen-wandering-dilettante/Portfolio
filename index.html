个人陈述 Statement

1999年12月生，现暂居湖南长沙，建筑及设计背景，关注个体在熟悉空间中的陌生感，以及在陌生空间中构建的在地感。 对「人在变动与迁移中如何安放自己」进行追问，并用创作与设计回应。实践以影像、诗歌、策展、服务设计、集体创作等多种形式展开。

Born in Dec 1999, currently based in Changsha, China, with an architecture & design background. I explore estrangement in familiar spaces and the making of situatedness in unfamiliar ones. My work asks: “How do we place ourselves amid movement and migration?” I respond through moving image, poetry, curatorial practice, service design, and collective making.
