Statement / 个人陈述

1999年12月生，现暂居湖南长沙，建筑及设计背景，关注个体在熟悉空间中的陌生感，以及在陌生空间中构建的在地感。

对「人在变动与迁移中如何安放自己」进行追问，并用创作与设计回应。实践以影像、诗歌、策展、服务设计、集体创作等多种形式展开。

可承接业务：设计研究 / 策展 / 影像创作 / 书籍设计 …

Born in Dec 1999, currently based in Changsha, China, with an architecture & design background.

I explore estrangement in familiar spaces and the making of situatedness in unfamiliar ones. My work asks: “How do we place ourselves amid movement and migration?” I respond through moving image, poetry, curatorial practice, service design, and collective making.

Available for: design research / curating / moving image / book design …
