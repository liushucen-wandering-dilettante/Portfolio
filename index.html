刘书岑

1999年12月生，现暂居湖南长沙，建筑及设计背景，关注个体在熟悉空间中的陌生感，以及在陌生空间中构建的在地感。

对「人在变动与迁移中如何安放自己」进行追问，并用创作与设计回应。 实践以影像、诗歌、策展、服务设计、集体创作等多种形式展开。 可承接业务：设计研究 / 策展 / 影像创作 / 书籍设计 …

Born in Dec 1999, currently based in Changsha, China, with an architecture & design background. I explore estrangement in familiar spaces and the making of situatedness in unfamiliar ones. My work asks: “How do we place ourselves amid movement and migration?” I respond through moving image, poetry, curatorial practice, service design, and collective making. Available for: design research / curating / moving image / book design …

2024.12–2026.02
叙室：支持过渡期青年叙事实践的产品服务系统 Narrative Room: A Product–Service System for Transitional Youth

服务设计 / 叙事工具 Service Design / Narrative Toolkit

为过渡期青年设计一套以卡牌为载体的叙事工具与服务流程，帮助用户梳理自我、建立连接。 构建包含空间场域、卡牌工具、主持脚本在内的完整服务系统，可灵活嵌入青年社区、共同工作空间等场景。

A card-based narrative toolkit and facilitation flow for transitional youth, supporting self-reflection and connection. The system includes the spatial setting, card toolkit, and facilitation script, adaptable to youth communities and co-working spaces.
